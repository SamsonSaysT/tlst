A list with receipts.

This is Tony’s collection of 63 original links, not an online store. Names were transcribed and cleaned up from the shared product screenshots, with brand-page cross-checks where available. Photos are cropped from those same screenshots. Seller claims are not independent test results.

How to read the prices

Brand ref. means the named manufacturer’s publicly displayed USD price was captured during the October 5, 2026 research pass. It is not a verified price at the original Amazon or AliExpress link. Versions, colors, sizes, bundles, discounts, tax and shipping can differ.

Shared price means a price visible in Tony’s screenshot or typed in a message. The date is preserved in product details. Check store means no reliable price was captured. No number was invented to fill the gap. Price sorting and pack subtotals mix clearly labeled references and snapshots; they are not shopping quotes.

What happened with the short links?

All 63 original short URLs were attempted in the research browser. The fetches were unavailable: the destination pages and current marketplace prices could not be independently confirmed. That does not establish that the links are broken. Every original link is preserved; open it in your own browser to check the exact variant and checkout price. Reference sources and any specific uncertainties are attached to each item.

Before you head out

This is a gear shortlist, not a complete trip plan. Check local conditions, carry navigation and the other essentials, and follow the instructions for filters, fuel stoves and shelters. Compare sleep-system ratings carefully; advertised warmth additions are not guarantees. Useful references: National Park Service planning checklist and Leave No Trace cleanup guidance.

Your own little pack

Heart a find to save it, tick items as packed, compare up to four products, and copy a list for your friends. Everything stays in your browser. Export a backup to move it to another device. No accounts, tracking scripts, or live price scraping. Click sounds start on after your first interaction; the speaker in the top bar mutes them. No background music.
